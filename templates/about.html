About KrishiKart

Fresh from Farm to your Home

Our Mission

KrishiKart connects local Nepali farmers directly with customers, cutting out middlemen so farmers earn more and customers pay less for genuinely fresh produce.

Why Choose Us

Every product is sourced directly from verified local farms. We support organic farming practices and fair pricing for the people who grow our food.

How It Works

Browse fresh produce, add to cart, and check out with Cash on Delivery or eSewa. Your order goes straight from the farmer's harvest to your doorstep.

Supporting Farmers

We believe farmers deserve fair compensation. That's why we built tools for farmers to manage their own listings and reach customers directly.
